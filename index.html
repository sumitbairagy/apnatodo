Privacy Policy

ApnaToDo · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ApnaToDo handles information when you use the ApnaToDo mobile application.

In short: Your to-do tasks and related task details are stored locally on your device. ApnaToDo does not require you to create an account. The app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements and Firebase Analytics to help us understand app usage. Those services may process certain device and usage information as described below.

1. Information You Enter and Store

When you use ApnaToDo, you may create tasks and enter information such as task titles, dates, times, reminders, and completion status. This information is stored in the app's local database on your device.

We do not operate a task-storage server for ApnaToDo, and task content is not intentionally sent to us through the app. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its app data may be able to access locally stored information.

2. How Your Local Data Is Used

Your locally stored task information is used to display and manage your task list, show pending or completed tasks, and support reminder or alarm features that you choose to use.

Your task data generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Device-level backup or restore settings may affect whether app data is backed up by your operating system; this is controlled by your device and its settings.

3. Notifications and Alarms

If you enable reminders or notifications, ApnaToDo may use notification or alarm permissions and local scheduling features to alert you at the times you choose. Reminder information is used to provide that feature. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Google AdMob

ApnaToDo uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location inferred from IP address, and ad interaction or diagnostic information. This information may be used to deliver, measure, and improve ads, including personalized advertising where permitted by your device settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

For information about how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Firebase Analytics

ApnaToDo uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand general app usage and improve app performance and features. Depending on the app configuration and your device settings, Analytics may collect information such as app interactions and events, device or app instance identifiers, device model, operating system, app version, approximate location, and technical or diagnostic information.

We use this information for analytics and app improvement. We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect the text of your to-do tasks intentionally. Learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security and Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your task content or personal information. Task information stored locally is not intentionally shared with us. Information may be processed by Google through AdMob and Firebase Analytics as described in this policy and under Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps in designing the app to keep task information within the app's local storage. However, no device or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep your operating system up to date.

8. Children's Privacy

ApnaToDo is a general productivity app and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through our own servers. Third-party advertising and analytics services may process device or usage information as described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, please contact us so we can review the concern.

9. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete tasks within the app.
  • You can clear ApnaToDo's local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data, subject to device backup behavior.
  • You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.
  • You can review or change advertising privacy settings and reset or limit your device's advertising identifier where those controls are available.
  • Some device or Google account settings may provide controls for Analytics and advertising personalization.

10. Third-Party Services

AdMob and Firebase Analytics are third-party services provided by Google. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and terms. ApnaToDo does not control the data practices of these third parties.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review it periodically.
